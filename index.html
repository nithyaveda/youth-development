📋 Planning Phase

From idea to action — the groundwork behind the transformation.

Fundraising

Root Beer Float Sale

We set up a booth on campus and sold Root Beer Floats at RM 5 per cup. Through teamwork, great customer service, and a lot of smiles, we successfully raised RM 250 to fund our community service project.

  • RM 5 per cup — affordable and appealing to students
  • RM 250 total raised to support community service
  • 2 days of sales to maximise outreach
  • Booth located at high-traffic campus area
  • Team members rotated shifts to cover all hours
  • Promoted via WhatsApp and word-of-mouth

Group Planning

Meetings & Role Assignment

We held multiple meetings to discuss our approach, assign roles, and ensure everyone had clear responsibilities. Each member was given a specific role based on their strengths and availability.

  • Assigned roles for fundraising (sales, finance, marketing)
  • Assigned roles for event day (media team, coordination)
  • Coordinated logistics and supply procurement
  • Established communication channels via WhatsApp
  • Discussed contingency plans for challenges
  • Prepared promotional materials and posters

Briefing

Official Event Briefing

On 13 August 2026, we attended the official briefing session with Dr. Shamsul Ammar and Madam Qusna, where we received detailed instructions on schedule, attire, committee roles, and safety protocols.

  • Schedule & timeline for the event day
  • Attire requirements (Panthera shirts, long pants, sport shoes)
  • Committee assignments (registration, logistics, F&B, media, emcee)
  • Explorace rules and checkpoint details
  • Safety and emergency protocols
  • Keep all receipts for claims

🌿 Event Day

16 August 2026 — A day of planting, painting, cleaning, and celebrating.

🌅
7:00 AM

Gathering at U Square

Students assembled at U Square for attendance registration. Attendance was taken before departure to Kebun Komuniti BBSA.

🚌

Welcome Speech & Opening Ceremony

Welcome speech by the Chairman of the Residents' Association, followed by the official opening ceremony.

✍️

Plaque Signing & Souvenir Presentation

Plaque signing ceremony and presentation of souvenirs by YB Azri, followed by a group photo session.

Breakfast

Participants enjoyed breakfast (nasi lemak) before the activities began.

📋
9:00 AM

MSU Programme Briefing

Dr. Shamsul Ammar briefed all students and committees on the day's activities, safety protocols, and Explorace rules.

🌱
9:15 AM – 11:30 AM

Core Activities (Concurrent)

Students rotated through four Explorace stations while also participating in community service tasks. The media team captured every moment.

🌱 Hydroponic Planting

Kebun BBSA staff taught students hydroponic techniques for planting kangkung and bok choy. Students learned about sustainable agriculture and planted seedlings.

🎨 Painting & Cleaning

Students painted the garden stones white to beautify the space, de-weeded the garden beds, and cleaned up the surrounding area.

🧹 Gotong-Royong

Students collected rubbish, swept pathways, and cleaned up the Platinum Housing Area, fostering community spirit through collective effort.

🇲🇾 Merdeka Pose

Teams creatively recreated meaningful poses with the Malaysian flag — including Tugu Negara, football free-kick wall formations, and other symbolic gestures celebrating unity and national pride.

Final group photo and video recording session capturing the spirit of the day.

🧹
12:30 PM

Students cleaned up the space and departed.
